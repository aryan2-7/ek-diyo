# Festival facts to double-check

The story cards are written in `src/data/nights.js`. I wrote them from general knowledge, so please check
each line with someone at home (or a trusted source) before submitting. Wording can be softened freely.

1. **Prologue:** Tihar lasts five days; it is also called Yamapanchak and Deepawali; the five honoured are
   the crow, the dog, the cow, oneself (Mha Puja) and brothers and sisters.
2. **Day 1, Kaag Tihar:** food (grain, sweets) is put out for crows; the crow is a messenger whose call can
   carry news, including sad news.
3. **Day 2, Kukur Tihar:** dogs get a marigold garland, a red tika and a meal; dogs are said to guard Yama's gate.
4. **Day 3, Gai Tihar and Laxmi Puja:** the cow is garlanded and honoured in the morning; in the evening homes are
   cleaned, lit with lamps, and Laxmi is said to visit bright homes.
5. **Day 4, Mha Puja:** the Newar community worships the self with a mandala drawn on the floor. (Other communities
   mark Govardhan Puja / ox worship on this day; the game only mentions Mha Puja.)
6. **Day 5, Bhai Tika:** sisters give brothers a seven-colour tika and pray for long life, with sweets, fruit and a garland;
   the Yamuna and Yama legend (Yama's boon of fearlessness for brothers who receive tika) is retold in one sentence.
7. **Game liberties** (not tradition): one more wick per night; the dog as a runner; the rooftop plates being "lit" to attract crows.
